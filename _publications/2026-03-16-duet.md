DUET is a disaggregated accelerator for hybrid Mamba–Transformer inference, with specialized packages for the prefill and decode phases. It uses configurable systolic arrays for prefill and configurable vector-unit arrays for decode.

**Accepted at the Design Automation Conference (DAC).** The linked paper is the arXiv version.

As the third author, I primarily contributed to **RTL design, synthesis, timing closure, and design space exploration (DSE)**.
